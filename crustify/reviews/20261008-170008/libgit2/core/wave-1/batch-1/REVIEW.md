Reviewed commit `4dfa6b86a` (wave base). The items landed in `2b63f91ae` (wave 1
batch 1): `feature_backend(Feature)` and `build_info(BuildInfo)` in
`crustify/rust/libgit2/libgit2/core/common.rs`, and `TraceFn` / `Tracer` /
`TraceCallback` (`new`, `unsafe from_fn`, `call`, `as_fn`, `raw`) in
`crustify/rust/libgit2/libgit2/core/trace.rs`. `git_buf_dispose` and
